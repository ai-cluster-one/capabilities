# lkagent — change log

## 2026-11-26 — lkagent: an agent's own phone line on LiveKit, GPT Live as the voice and the agent's own harness session as the backend

A new capability, `lkagent`, titled LiveKit Agent. `lkagent service run` registers a LiveKit worker under the line's agent name (the `agent_name` setting, else `lkagent-<connection id>`) and answers the calls dispatched to it, one at a time. GPT Live, under client delegation, is the voice; each turn it hands over is written at once into one live Claude process kept for the whole call (or a resumed Codex session), and the backend's steps reach the voice as thinking notes while it works. The call opens as `agent_speaks_first`, `greeting_type` and the greeting settings say, ends at `max_duration_seconds` or on silence, and a silent line is a backend turn told its instruction first and then what was said, all through callva-livekit, which also reports each call to `webhook_url`.

A connection is one LiveKit server: `url`, `api_key_env` and `secret_env`. The OpenAI key is the machine's `OPENAI_API_KEY` setup field, shared and resolved from the agent's vault first. A call is taken only on one of the line's `numbers`, each `{number, instructions, allowed_capabilities, read_only}`: an inbound call on the number it called, an outbound one on the number it is placed from, and the backend runs under that number's instructions and rights; a call on any other number is hung up at once and recorded as failed. `lkagent call <number>` dials through the `outbound_trunk` setting from `--from`, one of the line's numbers. Recording is off unless `recording` is set. Each finished call is a row of `lkagent_calls` in the project's store, read from the `call.ended` body; a project without a store records nothing and says so once. `doctor` and `inventory --network` read the SIP dispatch rules routing to the line and the numbers they route, and `doctor` warns when none does. The guide `twilio-number` connects a Twilio number to the line. The bundle carries LiveKit's mark as its `icon.png`, taken unaltered from LiveKit's GitHub organisation avatar (256 x 256); the mark is LiveKit's trademark, used only to identify the service the capability reaches.
