# teams — change log

## 2026-11-27 — `teams section` reads the store in a few round trips rather than about twenty

`teams section`, which `capabilities build` runs for every agent, asked the store whether step 0008 had run with `db.status` inside a transaction of its own - its opening, six reads one after another, and its commit - and then opened a read-only transaction apart, each step a round trip. It now asks the ledger which steps are applied in one read (`db.applied`, capabilities-contract 0.60), and reads the teams and their documents each as a read-only transaction of its own, read-only by a setting of the transaction rather than of the connection, so each read and its commit is one round trip. With capabilities-contract 0.60 the capability gate also reads the store's major, the agent's registry row and nodes, and whether the record tables need anything, in one round trip rather than three. With the store 100 ms away, `teams section` for an agent in no team went from 3.7 s to 1.9 s. What it prints is unchanged byte for byte, and on a store that has not taken step 0008 a team that was in ordinary mode still gives none of its always documents. The capability binds `capabilities-contract` 0.60 or later. No verb, flag, output or exit code changes.

## 2026-11-24 — `teams inventory` counts the teams this agent is in as a tile, and only with --network

`teams inventory` answered the empty envelope; the capability now declares its inventory `optional`. A bare run still opens no store: it names `teams` in `deferred`, as a count the store keeps. With --network it answers one metric, `teams`, with `kind` `total` and `glyph` `page`: the teams not retired that this agent is a member of, as `teams list` names them. Outside a project, or in one with no durable id, --network names the count in `deferred` and reaches no store; with no store configured or none reachable it refuses as every reading verb does, exit 6 `store_not_configured` or exit 5 `store_unreachable`. The capability binds `capabilities-contract` 0.53 or later, which supplies the tile helper. No other verb, flag, answer or exit code changes.

## 2026-11-23 — A team document can hold one draft: saving it adds no version, and publishing it adds one against its base, at teams 1.4

`teams doc draft save <team> <doc> --from FILE|- [--base ID]` keeps the file form as the document's one draft and adds no version; saving again writes over it and keeps its base, which is --base where given, else the draft's base, else the active version, and answers {team, document, outcome `created` or `saved`, changed, base, active, stale, saved_at, saved_by}. `teams doc draft show <team> <doc> [--json]` prints the draft, with --json {team, document, base, active, stale, saved_at, saved_by, content}; `teams doc draft discard <team> <doc>` removes it, answering {team, document, changed, base} (`"changed": false` with none); `teams doc draft publish <team> <doc> [--base ID] [--note TEXT]` saves the draft as `doc save` saves, as one new version against its base, removes it in the same transaction and answers what `doc save` answers, and refuses with exit 7 `stale_base`, naming the draft's base and the active version and keeping the draft, when another save came after the base; publishing again with --base naming the active version confirms the draft over it, and a draft equal to the active version adds no version and is removed. A draft is only of an existing document; none is held answers exit 3 `no_draft`. A draft is never read: `show`, `doc show`, `doc history` and `teams section` read versions only, so no member carries it. `show` marks a document with a draft, `(draft)` at the end of its plain line and `draft` in each JSON document entry. Draft save, discard and publish are writes, refused under the read-only switch as `doc save` is; draft show is a read. Step `0009-drafts` (teams 1.4, a minor step every process on the library applies on its own) adds `teams_drafts`, one row per document with a draft keyed by the document's id; a reader at 1.3 keeps working against 1.4. Every other verb, flag, answer and exit code is unchanged.

## 2026-11-23 — A document's own load decides what reaches a member; the team mode is gone, and a write that changes what members carry says what it costs them, at teams 1.3

A team no longer has a mode. A member's Teams section carries every enabled `always` document of each of its teams whole, under the team's name, and lists the enabled `on-request` ones by their line with the command that reads each; a team with no enabled `always` document is listed by its line and how to read its instructions, as an ordinary team was. `teams mode` and `teams create --always` are removed, and `mode` leaves the answers of `list`, `show` and `create` and the plain lines of `list` and `show`; `teams create` of an existing team now compares its line only.

The safety the mode gave moves onto the document. `teams doc save` and `teams doc on` answer `reach`: `agents`, the members of the team, and `tokens`, how many tokens the team's part of each member's Teams section gains, measured by composing it before and after the write in the same transaction (below zero where it shrinks); `doc save` also answers the version's `load`, and `doc on` the active version's `load`. `--dry-run` on both runs the same write, answers the same with `"dry_run": true` and a null `version` on a save, and rolls it back, so a switch to always shows its cost to every member before it lands.

Step `0008-load-decides` (teams 1.3, a minor step every process on the library applies on its own) keeps every member's context as it was: each `always` document, on or off and not retired, of a team that was in ordinary mode gets a new active version with its content unchanged and its load `on-request`, saved by `teams@step-0008` with a note saying why, so `teams doc history` shows the switch. `teams_teams.always` stays for readers before 1.3, which composed a team's always documents only where it is true: the step sets it true on every team and makes true its default, so such a reader sharing the store composes the always documents 1.3 composes; nothing in 1.3 reads it, and teams 2.0 drops it. Until a store has taken the step, `teams section`, which migrates nothing, composes a team that was in ordinary mode without its always documents, as 1.2 did, so installing this version changes no member's context before the step runs.

## 2026-11-22 — `teams help` is the menu, and `teams help <verb>` one verb's whole contract

`teams help` printed the whole reference, about 10.2 KB with flags on 19 of its lines, and `teams help <anything>`, an unknown word included, printed it again with exit 0. It is now the short complete menu of SHEBANG.md, "Agent-first help is the surface", about 5.6 KB: what a team and a document are, the two modes, the gates, where documents live, how a team or document is named, the file form, every verb on one line saying what it answers, the I/O and the exit codes, then the contract verbs the library renders, with no flag on any line. `teams help <verb>` prints that verb's whole contract from `HELP_VERBS` - its synopsis with every flag, the text the old help held for it, its output, exit codes and an example - for list, show, section, create, mode, rename, describe, retire, members add and remove, and doc show, history, save, on, off, rename and retire; `teams help doc` and `teams help members` list their verbs. IDS AND NAMES is on every page that takes a team or document, THE FILE FORM on `help doc show` and `help doc save`, SIZE on `help show`, `help doc show` and `help doc history`, the modes on `help create`, `help mode` and `help section`, and STORE on `help migrate`; the read and write headers' --json and read-only switch move to each read and write page. The old contract verbs' teams-specific notes follow the library's pages of doctor, connections, manifest, guide, inventory and migrate. An unknown path exits 6 `unknown_verb` with nothing on stdout and the hint to run `teams help`. The capability binds `capabilities-contract` 0.50 or later, which dispatches the layered help. No verb, flag, answer, JSON key or exit code changes.

## 2026-11-09 — `teams section` prints an agent's Teams section, and the declaration names it

The new verb `teams section` prints the Teams section of the context of the agent it runs in, as Markdown: a level-1 `Teams` heading, the lines that say the teams bind the agent and that its own doctrine wins on conflict, and each team it is a member of under a level-2 heading with its line, with how to read an ordinary team's instructions and an always team's enabled always documents whole under level-3 headings, their own headings shifted two levels deeper outside code, and its enabled on-request documents as one level-3 group. The manifest's `contribution` adds `"command": ["section"]` and `"place": "standing"`, so a capabilities manager of this release composes the section from it, ahead of the agent's own doctrine; a manager of an earlier release refuses this declaration at install, as it refuses any key it does not know, and keeps the teams payload it has. ## 2026-11-09 — A team and a document each have an id that never changes, and a name that can, at teams 1.2

Step `0007-ids` (teams 1.2, a minor step every process on the library applies on its own) gives every team and every document an `id`, twelve lowercase hex characters minted once, which the store refuses to change. `teams_teams.id` and `teams_documents.id` are the primary keys; `teams_members.team_id` and `teams_documents.team_id` reference `teams_teams (id)` and `teams_versions.document_id` references `teams_documents (id)`; the keys on names are gone. A name is free text: one line, not empty, no space at either end, at most 128 characters, and not shaped like an id; a team's is unique in the store (`teams_teams_name_key`), a document's within its team. `teams_teams` and `teams_documents` gain `retired_at`. The text columns `teams_members.team`, `teams_documents.team`, `teams_versions.team` and `teams_versions.document` stay, with no key, as copies of the current name that the store fills on insert - also from a writer that gives only the name - and carries through a rename in the same transaction, so a reader or writer released before ids keeps working; teams 2.0 drops them. The versions guard admits changes to those copies and to `active` only. Every team, member, document and version a store holds survives the step.

New verbs: `teams rename <team> <name>`, `teams describe <team> --description TEXT`, `teams retire <team>`, `teams doc rename <team> <doc> <name>` and `teams doc retire <team> <doc>`; a name another team or document holds is refused with exit 6 `name_taken`. Retiring removes a team's members or switches a document off, lists it no more and frees its name, which then carries its id; its versions stay and its id still reads it. Every verb takes a team or document by its id or its current name. `list`, `show`, `create`, `doc show` and `doc history` answer the ids (`id`, `team_id`, `document_id`) beside the names; no key is removed. A team or document name is no longer limited to the old slug characters, so `create "Crew!"` is now accepted where it was refused.

## 2026-11-08 — teams: teams of agents that carry shared instructions in the store

A new core capability. A team is a store record: a name, one line saying what it is for and when to turn to it, its members (agents, by declared id), and versioned instruction documents, each marked `always` or `on-request` and each switched on or off for the team. A team is in ordinary mode (the default), where a member's context lists it by its line, or in always mode, where its enabled always documents are composed whole into every member's context under the team's name and its enabled on-request documents are listed by their line. The capabilities manager composes that part at build straight from the store; the manifest's `contribution` is the constant half and carries nothing that changes between sessions. A team gives knowledge only: no connection, key, capability or grant.

Verbs: `teams list [--all]`, `teams show <team>`, `teams create <team> --description TEXT [--always]`, `teams mode <team> ordinary|always`, `teams members add|remove <team> <agent>`, `teams doc show|history <team> <doc>`, `teams doc save <team> <doc> --from FILE|- [--base ID] [--note TEXT]` (refused with exit 7 `stale_base` where the base is no longer active), and `teams doc on|off <team> <doc>`. `show` and `doc show --json` give each document's size in tokens, estimated as a quarter of its characters, and a team's `tokens` and `always_tokens`. Writes are refused under the read-only switch. The tables `teams_teams`, `teams_members`, `teams_documents` and `teams_versions` are migrated under the `teams` ledger at 1.0; versions are immutable but for `active`, and never deleted. Declares `capabilities-contract>=0.43,<1`.
