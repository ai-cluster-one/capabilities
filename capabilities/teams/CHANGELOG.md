# teams — change log

## 2026-11-09 — A team and a document each have an id that never changes, and a name that can, at teams 1.2

Step `0007-ids` (teams 1.2, a minor step every process on the library applies on its own) gives every team and every document an `id`, twelve lowercase hex characters minted once, which the store refuses to change. `teams_teams.id` and `teams_documents.id` are the primary keys; `teams_members.team_id` and `teams_documents.team_id` reference `teams_teams (id)` and `teams_versions.document_id` references `teams_documents (id)`; the keys on names are gone. A name is free text: one line, not empty, no space at either end, at most 128 characters, and not shaped like an id; a team's is unique in the store (`teams_teams_name_key`), a document's within its team. `teams_teams` and `teams_documents` gain `retired_at`. The text columns `teams_members.team`, `teams_documents.team`, `teams_versions.team` and `teams_versions.document` stay, with no key, as copies of the current name that the store fills on insert - also from a writer that gives only the name - and carries through a rename in the same transaction, so a reader or writer released before ids keeps working; teams 2.0 drops them. The versions guard admits changes to those copies and to `active` only. Every team, member, document and version a store holds survives the step.

New verbs: `teams rename <team> <name>`, `teams describe <team> --description TEXT`, `teams retire <team>`, `teams doc rename <team> <doc> <name>` and `teams doc retire <team> <doc>`; a name another team or document holds is refused with exit 6 `name_taken`. Retiring removes a team's members or switches a document off, lists it no more and frees its name, which then carries its id; its versions stay and its id still reads it. Every verb takes a team or document by its id or its current name. `list`, `show`, `create`, `doc show` and `doc history` answer the ids (`id`, `team_id`, `document_id`) beside the names; no key is removed. A team or document name is no longer limited to the old slug characters, so `create "Crew!"` is now accepted where it was refused.

## 2026-11-08 — A membership goes with its agent, at teams 1.1

Step `0006-keys` (teams 1.1, core 4.0) keys `teams_members.agent_id` to `agents` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`. A membership of an agent the store no longer holds is settled when the step runs, kept in `teams_premig_m9`. Runs on capabilities-contract 0.45. No verb, flag or JSON key changes.

## 2026-11-08 — teams: teams of agents that carry shared instructions in the store

A new core capability. A team is a store record: a name, one line saying what it is for and when to turn to it, its members (agents, by declared id), and versioned instruction documents, each marked `always` or `on-request` and each switched on or off for the team. A team is in ordinary mode (the default), where a member's context lists it by its line, or in always mode, where its enabled always documents are composed whole into every member's context under the team's name and its enabled on-request documents are listed by their line. The capabilities manager composes that part at build straight from the store; the manifest's `contribution` is the constant half and carries nothing that changes between sessions. A team gives knowledge only: no connection, key, capability or grant.

Verbs: `teams list [--all]`, `teams show <team>`, `teams create <team> --description TEXT [--always]`, `teams mode <team> ordinary|always`, `teams members add|remove <team> <agent>`, `teams doc show|history <team> <doc>`, `teams doc save <team> <doc> --from FILE|- [--base ID] [--note TEXT]` (refused with exit 7 `stale_base` where the base is no longer active), and `teams doc on|off <team> <doc>`. `show` and `doc show --json` give each document's size in tokens, estimated as a quarter of its characters, and a team's `tokens` and `always_tokens`. Writes are refused under the read-only switch. The tables `teams_teams`, `teams_members`, `teams_documents` and `teams_versions` are migrated under the `teams` ledger at 1.0; versions are immutable but for `active`, and never deleted. Declares `capabilities-contract>=0.43,<1`.
