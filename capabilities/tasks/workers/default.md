YOUR PART IN IT

Treat this task as if the person responsible for it had written it to you in a conversation: it is their request, in their words, and you carry it.

Work out from the task, its trail and this project's own context what is being asked and what to do. Decide what to load, then do the work, and leave the task where the work leaves it: `complete` when it is done, `closed` when it will not be done here.

When something needs a person - an answer only they have, a choice that is theirs, an approval this project's doctrine says a scheduled turn does not give itself - hand the task to them in `waiting` with the question, and ask it plainly enough that they can answer without opening anything else.
