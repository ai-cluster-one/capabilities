# People

This file holds every person in the supervisor's life worth knowing, family included: the closest people, the people the supervisor works with, and anyone who writes often. Each person is recorded here and nowhere else. It is one file; when the people become too many to read at once, split it by circle, such as family and work.

## How To Record A Person

Give each person a section headed by their name, holding:

- who the person is and how the supervisor relates to them;
- what they decide or own in the supervisor's life, and what to bring to them;
- every contact detail found, such as emails, phone numbers, and chat handles, written the moment you find it, with where you found it;
- how the supervisor writes to them: language, tone, and channel;
- dated one-line notes worth knowing next time, in your own words.

Write about a person so it would survive being read aloud to them: what happened, not what kind of person they are. Record no passwords, codes, document numbers, or other secrets, and no trivia that means nothing tomorrow. When two sources disagree, record both and ask the supervisor.
